2. THE HURDLE (|t| needed for a 5% false-alarm rate, two-sided)
   classic single test:                      t > 1.96
   'p < 0.005' (Benjamin et al. 2018):        t > 2.81   <- this is where 2.8 comes from
   Harvey-Liu-Zhu 2016 (finance factor zoo): t > 3.00
   Bonferroni for   13 trials:              t > 2.89
   Bonferroni for  116 trials:              t > 3.52
   Bonferroni for  624 trials:              t > 3.94
   Bonferroni for 1000 trials:              t > 4.06
